## Telemetry volume

| signal | GiB/day | reducible by sampling | note |
|---|---:|---|---|
| traces at 1% head sampling | 0.045 | yes | 4.47 GiB/day unsampled; the sampler is the only lever |
| structured logs | 2.515 | yes | reducible by exclusion filters, at the cost of the investigation path |
| audit records | 1.863 | **no** | not sampleable; this is the evidence a dispute or an auditor asks for |
| metrics | 0.004 | **no** | 69 declared series; bounded by the catalogue, not by traffic |

Of 4.427 GiB/day, 2.56 GiB/day can be reduced by sampling and 1.867 GiB/day cannot. The metric line is flat in traffic: 69 series regardless of how many payments arrive.

## Negative controls

A catalogue written by the same hand as the implementation proves nothing
until each scenario has been shown to fail when the mechanism it tests is
removed.

| mechanism removed | scenarios that turn red | targeted |
|---|---|---|
| no-invariant-checker | S01, S03, S04, S05, S13, S14, S15, S16, S17 | yes |
| no-state-age-check | S01, S03, S17 | yes |
| no-redaction | S08 | yes |
| no-cardinality-guard | S09 | yes |
| no-audit-trail | S07 | yes |
| disable-everything | S01, S03, S04, S05, S07, S08, S09, S13, S14, S15, S16, S17 | **rejected** |

The blunt mutation is rejected on purpose: a mutation that reddens most of the catalogue cannot show which scenario covers which layer.
