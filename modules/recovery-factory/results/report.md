Generated by `make report`. No cloud project, no credentials, no network.

All figures are **simulated results**. RTO and RPO are measured inside the model
and are not vendor figures or targets.

## Protection against the operator

| Action by a fully privileged identity | Result |
|---|---|
| delete the locked artifact | `RETENTION_POLICY_NOT_MET` |
| shorten its retention | refused — locked retention policy cannot be reduced |
| artifact state afterwards | `RETAINED` |

Modelled on a locked retention policy, which "cannot be removed or reduced".
Protection is a ratchet: extending retention is always allowed, shortening never is.
