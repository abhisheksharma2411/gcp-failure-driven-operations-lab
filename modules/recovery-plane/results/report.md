## The shape this reproduces

Meta, 5 October 2021: "the total loss of DNS broke many of the internal
tools we'd normally use to investigate and resolve outages like this".

`restore-dns` reaches its bastion through IAP, and IAP needs DNS. The step
cannot run at the only time it is needed. Nobody designed that; it is a
property of a graph nobody drew.
